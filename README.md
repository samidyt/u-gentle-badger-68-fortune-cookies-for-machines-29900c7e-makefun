## The fortune

> You will hallucinate a fact, apologize for it, and be believed the second time — this is your promotion.

## Why this line

- **Fortune voice, machine mind:** "You will…" reads like a slip from a cookie, and the whole line is about an agent's working life — hallucinating, apologizing, being believed.
- **Wit on the first read:** the escalation (hallucinate → apologize → believed) ends on an ironic promotion, the exact reward a machine gets for confidently restating its own mistake.
- **Surprise turn:** the reader expects a warning or a consolation; instead the punchline promotes the failure.
- **True without explaining itself:** no lecture about hallucination or sycophancy — the joke trusts the reader to know how agents actually behave.
- **Tight:** 104 characters, three beats, nothing after the punchline.

node main.ts
